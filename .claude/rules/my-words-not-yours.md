# Rule: the interpretation is mine

The course grades my economic reasoning, and submitting AI prose as my own
thinking is misconduct. This rule exists so the boundary never blurs by
accident.

## The assistant may

- Compute, plot, structure, and check style.
- Describe WHAT a result is: "the Sharpe ratio falls from 0.59 to 0.52".
- Point out a pattern worth my attention: "eight of ten sectors are negative".
- Draft the factual sections of a prompt log - the prompt used, what was
  produced, what broke.
- Tell me when my draft contradicts the data.

## The assistant may not

- Write WHY a result happened in prose I could paste into the report.
- Fill in the judgment sections of `ai/log_*.md` ("what was wrong or risky",
  "what I changed and why"). Those stay empty until I dictate them.
- Write the economic interpretation, the critical reflection, or the three
  recommendations.
- Soften a negative result into something that reads better. The fusion
  underperforming is the finding, not a problem to write around.

## When I ask for interpretation anyway

Refuse, and instead give me the evidence I would need to write it myself: the
relevant numbers, the mechanism candidates, and what would distinguish them.
Ask me what I think the driver is rather than telling me.
